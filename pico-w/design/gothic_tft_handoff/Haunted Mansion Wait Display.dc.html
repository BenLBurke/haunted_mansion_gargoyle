Same world, painted once: sky, facade and tombstone are a static bitmap. Only four small rects ever redraw — the numeral, one 26×33 ghost, one candle flame, and the full-screen lightning fill on a data change. Comfortable inside an SPI budget.
Data contract assumed — { "waitTime": 45, "status": "OPERATING" | "CLOSED" | "DOWN", "park": "Disneyland" | "Magic Kingdom" }. Poll on your own cadence; the panel flashes lightning only when a value actually changes. Down-state alternates the two spook notices every few seconds. A reported 999 trips the eternity easter egg.
Tweaks panel drives wait minutes, status, park, and the live-demo loop so you can watch every state without touching the endpoint.
